## Root Cause

The recommendation engine weighted broad genres too heavily.

"Sports" grouped together:

-   Street Racing
-   Soccer
-   Volleyball
-   Boxing
-   Figure Skating
-   Cycling
-   Baseball
-   Basketball

These share almost none of the same viewing experience.
